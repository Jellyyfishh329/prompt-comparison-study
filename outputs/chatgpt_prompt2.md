Imagine teaching a computer to recognize cats by showing it thousands of cat pictures. That's basically how a **Neural Network** works!

A neural network is a computer system inspired by the human brain. It learns from examples and uses what it has learned to make predictions or decisions.

Here's how it works:

1\. **Input Layer:** The network receives information. For example, when you upload a cat picture, it takes in the image data.

2\. **Hidden Layers:** These layers process the information. They look for patterns such as shapes, edges, ears, and eyes. Each connection has a value called a *weight*, which determines how important that information is.

3\. **Output Layer:** After processing everything, the network produces an answer, such as "This is a cat."

4\. **Learning:** Initially, the network may make mistakes. It compares its prediction with the correct answer and adjusts its weights to improve. By repeating this process with many examples, it becomes more accurate.

**One-line summary:** A neural network learns from examples by finding patterns, making predictions, and correcting its mistakes.
